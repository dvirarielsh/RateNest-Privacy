Privacy Policy for RateNest

Effective date: October 8, 2026

RateNest is an Android application providing VAT and investment calculation tools. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app. It should be reviewed against the released app version and updated if its functionality or third-party services change.

Information you enter

RateNest processes the numbers and settings you enter to calculate results. The app is designed to maintain calculation history and preferences on your device. These values are not intended to be sent to the developer's servers. Removing the app or clearing its storage may delete locally saved information. Android backup features, if enabled and supported by the app, may separately back up app data under your device's settings.

Advertising and third-party services

RateNest may display ads through Google AdMob in versions where advertising is enabled. When enabled, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, approximate location inferred from IP address, and ad performance or diagnostic information. They may use this information to serve, measure, and protect advertising, subject to applicable consent choices and law. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

The presence and behavior of advertising depend on the specific version of RateNest you install. Test ads used during development are not necessarily present in the public release.

Sharing and retention

We do not intend to sell calculation entries or operate a server that receives your calculation history. Where advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may process and share advertising-related information according to their own policies. Locally stored calculation history and preferences generally remain until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall it, subject to device backup behavior. Third-party retention periods are governed by those providers.

Your choices

You can clear RateNest's app data or uninstall it to remove locally stored information. Your Android device and Google account may provide controls for advertising personalization, privacy, and consent. Where required, the app should present applicable consent choices before advertising-related processing.

Children

RateNest is a general-purpose financial calculator and is not specifically directed to children. Advertising and age-related safeguards must be configured according to the actual audience and applicable Google Play policies.

Security

We aim to minimize collection of calculation data. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Third-party services are responsible for their own data handling and security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app, its services, or legal requirements. The effective date above will be revised when the policy changes.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, you can contact the developer through the RateNest-Privacy GitHub issue tracker. GitHub account access may be required to submit an issue. A dedicated contact email may be added here before publication.
